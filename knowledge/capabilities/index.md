- [Soulseek protocol codec](soulseek-protocol.md) — Frame reader and client messages through chat, matched to Nicotine+ 3.3.11.
- [Terminal UI](terminal-ui.md) — Ratatui shell with a sign-in form, braille meters, eleven views, and a vertical scrollbar on every long list.
